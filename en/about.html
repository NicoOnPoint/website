ABOUT NICO ON POINT SERVICES

Local knowledge, practical help and one clear point of contact.

Nico on Point Services is built around a simple idea: when something needs doing, checking or solving in Värmland, you should know who is actually going to be there.

PERSONAL & PRACTICAL

A small business with a broad practical role.

Based in Lesjöfors, Barbara and I help homeowners, holiday-home owners, buyers and small businesses with practical work. Having a reliable local contact makes it easier to get things organised, including when you are not in Sweden yourself.

I focus on repairs, installation, technical maintenance, Home Checks and practical coordination around rentals. Barbara helps maintain and care for holiday homes and takes responsibility for Värmlands Private Dining. Our work complements each other, with personal attention to both the property and the people staying there.

Our approach is to think practically, communicate clearly and agree on the work in advance. We are honest about what we can do ourselves and when an authorised specialist is needed. Through my network, I can help find the right tradespeople.

HOW WE WORK

Clear expectations before the work starts.

01

Direct communication

You speak with the person who visits the property and does the work. Fewer handovers means fewer misunderstandings.

02

Practical solutions

We look for sensible solutions that fit the house, the situation and the budget instead of making a small problem unnecessarily complicated.

03

Photo updates

For remote owners and buyers, photos and concise updates make it easier to stay in control without being physically present.

04

Hands-on work, specialists when needed

I can carry out most practical work myself. If part of the job requires specific authorisation or certification, I discuss this in advance and can involve the appropriate tradespeople through my network and coordinate the practical arrangements.

BASED IN VÄRMLAND

Useful when Sweden is home — or when it is far away.

A large part of the service is useful for people who own, rent out or are considering buying property in Sweden while living somewhere else. A local contact can visit, check, photograph, coordinate and solve the smaller issues before they become bigger ones.

We work from the Lesjöfors area and take on jobs in Värmland and, depending on the assignment, beyond.
