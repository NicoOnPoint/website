WHAT I CAN HELP WITH

I design and build custom websites using a range of software tools and online platforms. The websites are optimised for search engines (SEO) and for use on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

A website should make it easy for visitors to understand what you offer and how to contact you. I help turn your services, photos and information into a clear structure that works on computers, tablets and phones.

01

Business websites

Pages that explain your services, show your work and make contact details easy to find.

02

Portfolio and project websites

03

Website updates

Changes to text, images and page structure, with the scope agreed before work begins.

04

Domains and hosting

Practical help with domain and hosting setup, or with understanding what needs to be changed on an existing website.

A clear plan before the work starts.

Tell me what you need, where you are based and any practical requirements. We agree on the scope and a price indication before work starts. If further work becomes necessary, I discuss it with you first.

Send your current website address if you have one, a short description of your business and what you want visitors to do. Existing photos, text and examples help us agree on the right scope. Any third-party costs for domains, hosting or paid services are discussed separately.
