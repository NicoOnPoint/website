An additional service for your holiday property

Värmlands Private Dining is an additional service that holiday-home owners can offer their guests. Refer interested guests to us; we discuss the menu, price, dietary requirements and a suitable date with them.

We agree with you in advance how the service can be delivered at your property, including kitchen facilities, access and any house rules. Reservations are confirmed after the price, arrangements and availability have been agreed.

Your host and chef

Barbara is your host and chef, looking after your evening with warmth and personal care. Her love of cooking began at the age of four. With a passion for flavours and aromas, she enjoys cooking for small groups. For Barbara, food is more than a meal: it is about enjoying time together, slowing down and making happy memories.

A catered 3-course dinner on location

The service is tailored to your holiday property and the group. The dinner includes the following tasks.

  • Agreeing on the menu with guests
  • Buying ingredients as agreed
  • Preparing and cooking at the holiday property
  • Serving and coordinating the courses
  • Tidying the kitchen afterwards, included in the service

Price on request

For each dinner, we prepare a proposal based on the menu, guest numbers, dietary requirements and location. Your guests receive a clear price before making a reservation. Travel costs are included. All prices include moms (Swedish VAT).

Guest reservations

Please ask your guests to book their dinner at least 5 days in advance, giving Barbara enough time to prepare. A booking is confirmed once we have confirmed availability and the arrangements with the guests.
