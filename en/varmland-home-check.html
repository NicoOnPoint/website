WHY HOME CHECK?

A second pair of eyes in Sweden.

Online listings can tell you a lot, but they rarely show everything you want to know before travelling a long distance to view a house.

With Home Check Värmland, I can visit the property on your behalf and give you a practical impression of the house, the visible condition and the immediate surroundings.

HOW IT WORKS

From listing to a clearer picture.

1

Send the property

Share the listing and tell me which questions or concerns are most important to you.

2

I visit the location

I look at the property and surroundings from a practical point of view and document what is visible.

3

You receive feedback

You get photos, video and clear observations so you can decide whether the property is worth the next step.

WHAT I CAN LOOK AT

Practical details that matter.

✓

Exterior

Roofline, façade, windows, access, visible wear and the general state around the building.

✓

Interior

Visible condition of rooms, floors, walls, kitchen, bathroom and other areas that can be accessed.

✓

Practical systems

Visible heating, water and electrical arrangements, where these can be observed without specialist testing.

✓

Surroundings

Access roads, neighbouring environment, terrain and other practical impressions around the property.

✓

Your questions

If something in the listing is unclear, I can pay extra attention to that point during the visit.

✓

Photo & video record

Extra visual material can help you compare the listing with what the property actually looks like on site.

A HOME CHECK IN PRACTICE

More than a few listing photos.

Real examples of the kind of details, interiors and surroundings that can be documented during a viewing.

CLEAR FEEDBACK

Useful information, without making it complicated.

The goal is to give you a realistic first impression and highlight visible practical points that may deserve more attention before you book a trip, make an offer or arrange a professional inspection.

Important: Home Check is practical viewing support and is not a certified Swedish building inspection (överlåtelsebesiktning) or specialist technical assessment. Where a formal inspection is needed, that should be carried out by the appropriate qualified professional.
