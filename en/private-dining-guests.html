Your host and chef

Payment and cancellation policy

All Private Dining bookings are paid in full in advance.

If you need to cancel your booking, the following refund policy applies:

  • More than 7 days before the booked date: 100% refund
  • 7 days to 72 hours before the booked date: 75% refund
  • 72 to 24 hours before the booked date: 50% refund
  • Less than 24 hours before the booked date: no refund
  • No-show: no refund

As our Private Dining service requires advance planning, ingredient purchasing and preparation, cancellation costs increase closer to the booked date.

Any applicable refund will be returned to the customer after cancellation.
